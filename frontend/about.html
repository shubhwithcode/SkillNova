ABOUT SKILLNOVA

Smart Education — AI-Based Skill & Placement Learning Platform

SkillNova is built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 to solve a real problem: skill development programs are often not aligned with what the industry and the job market actually demand.

Problem Statement · SIH26134

Challenges in aligning skill development programs with industry requirements and emerging job market demands.

Team · TIMSCDR07

Developed by students of TIMSCDR, MCA Batch E, combining web development, databases and applied AI.

Our Tagline

Empowering students with personalized, AI-driven placement preparation.

Our mission

Every student deserves a clear, personalized path to their first job. SkillNova analyzes your target role, your current skills and your resume, then builds a learning roadmap, gives actionable resume feedback and lets you practice interviews — so you always know exactly what to do next and how ready you are.

TECHNOLOGY

Built with an industry-standard stack

Simple, understandable technologies chosen for learning and reliability.

Frontend

HTML5, CSS3 and modern JavaScript (ES6+) with responsive, accessible layouts.

Backend & Database

Node.js with Express.js REST APIs and a normalized MySQL database.

AI & Python

Google Gemini for intelligent feedback and Python for PDF resume text extraction.
